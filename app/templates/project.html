What will this start?

One job for the whole upload. Each file is laid out, read, coded and written up; then the themes are grouped over everything, a line of claims is written per theme per material, and the theme accounts and project summary are written last. The number of model calls grows with how much you add, and you can leave the page while it runs. More in the guide.
