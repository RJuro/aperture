# T1–T4 close-reading review

## Scope and method

This review covers all 110 consolidated crosswalk rows for T1–T4, each complete source transcript, the original C1/C2/C3 initial-code records (including annotations and memos), and all 450 machine claims for these four interviews. It is a close reading, not a label-string, regex, or embedding comparison. A human meaning was treated as supported when the excerpt and its local transcript context warrant it; different coder emphases were retained together when each was defensible. `machine_claim_ids` in the JSON are provenance anchors to AI claims on the aligned passage, not evidence of a scored similarity decision.

The revised judgments are 90 substantive matches, five partial/different-lens cases, and 15 human insights missing from AI claims. AI claims were re-checked beyond the rows marked `machine_covered`; 15 consolidated rows have no linked machine claim. Several initial apparent misses were crosswalk false negatives: T2’s playful imitation of Ellis Island screening (row 45), ship curiosity (row 57), California/college move (row 56), and shop (row 63); T3’s fever separation (row 68), Detroit finances (row 72), anti-Jewish assault (row 87), and cousin reconnection (row 89); and T4’s year-long family separation (row 104). The 450-claim corpus is much more atomized than the human coding: it offers useful contextual observations, but frequently turns one passage into several descriptive claims.

## Provenance and interpretation notes

The crosswalk’s `machine_covered` status should not decide the evaluation. It is useful for locating linked claims, but it misses substantive AI coverage outside its lexical alignment (for example, T2 row 45’s agency, T3 row 68’s separation, and T4 row 104’s family separation). It also misses human themes that remain absent after the full claim check (for example, T1’s derivative citizenship and much of T3’s later educational/vocational self-account). Conversely, a linked AI claim may merely restate an event. The review calls a substantive match only where that event supports the human thematic meaning in context.

AI-only insights are most useful when they expose structure that the initial codes do not foreground: T1’s mediated childhood memory (row 41), the institutional organization of Ellis Island separation, and the pattern of ethnic institutions in T4. They become trivial when they split a single fact into many claims (for example, separate claims for ice cream, a tag, a bed, or each stage of a reunion). They overreach when a claim adds a causal mechanism not spoken by the participant: T1 AI language claims infer English-immersion causation from rapid school promotion and later study difficulties, while the interview gives no direct language-learning account. The JSON retains claim IDs so these distinctions can be audited passage by passage.
