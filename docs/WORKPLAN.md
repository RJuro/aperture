# Workplan — Aperture to a deployed, universal instrument

*2026-09-02. Ponytail throughout: the shortest working diff, delete before adding, no
abstraction with one caller. Subagents are Opus only. Codex (gpt-5.6-sol, luna as fallback) is
local-only: it indexes, condenses and reviews; it never ships and never touches a deployment.*

## What "done" means

A researcher opens a URL, signs in with an account an admin made for them, starts a project,
drops in files of several kinds, and reads the analysis: material pages with claims beside the
text they rest on, theme pages across the corpus, a corpus summary, an export. Every quote is
verified. Every number is a derivation. No JavaScript. It looks like an instrument someone was
proud to make.

## Rules that hold across every phase

- Law 5. A prompt template is universal; a slot holds material, structure, or the researcher's
  words. `tests/test_p9_law5.py` runs on every change.
- Our vocabulary stays out of every page: `context._BANNED`.
- Every phase ends with a real-output check, not a green suite.
- Every subagent owns named files and touches nothing else; the contract tests are written by
  me before it starts.
- Codex is local-only. Nothing it produces is deployed without my reading it.
